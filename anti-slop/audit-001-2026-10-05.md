## 1. Executive Summary
The redesign replaces the standard vertical projects grid with an interactive, cinematic sports-editorial showcase inspired by `jjettas.com`. The transition triggers upon scrolling past the final Journey panel, featuring a **continuous scroll-scrubbing engine** where each scroll notch advances progress incrementally (requiring multiple scrolls for full reveal). An angled black backdrop (`clip-path: polygon(0 clamp(260px, 22vw, 360px), 100% 0, 100% 100%, 0 100%)`) gracefully rises from the very bottom of the viewport with the right side dramatically higher than the left (300px+ height difference). Card 01 (PocketLog) emerges continuously from the bottom-left corner while flipping on a 3D Y-axis (`rotateY: -260deg -> 0deg`) before settling into the horizontal historical timeline accompanied by a precision football field yard lines ruler (YARD 10 to 60). Prior to reaching the final Journey slide, the Projects layer is completely hidden from the viewport.
